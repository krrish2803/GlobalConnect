Good to know

A few things you might be wondering.

Here’s what the GlobalConnect prototype demonstrates today.

What is GlobalConnect?

GlobalConnect is a prototype concept for order-linked marketplace conversations, translated messaging, contact-safety checks, and structured dispute workflows.

Is the chat connected to a live messaging service?

No. The current workspace and conversation are a product demo. The repository does not yet connect to a live Nexconn chat service.

Are translations generated by a live AI provider?

No. The demo API has a small sample phrase dictionary and placeholder output for other messages. Live translation and cultural AI integrations are planned, not implemented.

Does GlobalConnect process real payments or hold funds?

No. The escrow flow is a sandbox simulation with sample order data. It does not move, receive, or protect real money.

How do I enter the demo?

Choose Explore the demo or Try the conversation demo, create an account, or sign in to continue to the workspace.
