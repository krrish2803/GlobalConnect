The Challenge

The Cross-Border Language Barrier Crisis

Cross-border e-commerce is a $4 Trillion market, yet communication breakdown causes catastrophic loss in conversions and trust.

Critical Market Vulnerability

33% of consumers across the Middle East and Africa cite language as their absolute #1 barrier when making online purchases. When buyers cannot comfortably ask questions about specifications, shipping times, or warranties, 15%+ abandon their carts and over 45% of cross-border transactions end in disputes.

$23.5B
Machine Translation Market Gap

Poor Translation Quality

Traditional machine translators translate word-for-word, stripping nuance, technical slang, and commercial terms. Sellers either abandon non-English leads or make commitments that don't match reality.

Direct Consequence High Miscommunication

Cultural Misalignment

Formality levels, sarcasm, business etiquette (such as Japanese Keigo or Arabic honorifics), and negotiation expectations are misread as rude or evasive, alienating buyers.

Direct Consequence 15%+ Cart Abandonment

Zero Trust & Escrow Friction

Standard chats lack embedded transactional context. Disagreements over condition or delivery spiral out of control because there is no neutral arbiter or integrated fund escrow.

Direct Consequence 45% International Disputes
